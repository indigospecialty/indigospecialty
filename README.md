# Indigo Specialty Products

**Specialty plant care, developed for Australian conditions · Rocklea, Queensland**

Indigo Specialty Products Pty Ltd is a privately owned Australian supplier working in specialty plant care. Its compact team has backgrounds in manufacturing, distribution, product development and product registration. It develops plant protection, weed and pest control, plant nutrition, soil, water management and biological products for non-crop and niche horticultural users across Australasia. Production stays in Australia where it can, and formulations go through local field trials so they suit Australian conditions.

## Who we work with

- Turf & Amenity
- Nursery Production
- Industrial Vegetation Management
- Forestry
- Aquatics
- Pest Control
- Consumer Home Garden & Pest Management
